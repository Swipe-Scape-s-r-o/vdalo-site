# Homepage redesign, 24 October 2026

## Confirmed brief

Shorten the VDALO home, improve visual interest, purposeful motion and the path to a lead. Apply the user-selected Emil Kowalski, Impeccable and Taste skills. Preserve the plum/pink identity, logo, manual onboarding, prices, existing routes and delivery of forms. Do not publish free initial setup. No U+2014 in public copy. Staging precedes owner approval and main.

## Product and audience

VDALO is hotel operations software for hotel owners and teams. Visitors should understand bookings, guest preparation and team workflows, then request a demonstration. The team responds to requests and helps with setup. Prices and module availability come from pricing-core.js. AI assistant, PRRO and other development modules cannot be advertised as released.

Impeccable launcher unavailable; no concept board or approved comp. Existing user instructions authorize execution without another direction question.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Motion purpose

Arrival state enters once to explain readiness. The hero photograph settles slowly once. Selected section parts enter briefly, with short stagger. Controls respond to press. Calendar data and keyboard-driven tab changes remain immediate. Respect reduced motion, pause the image when the tab is hidden, keep HTML visible if scripts fail. No new animation dependency.

- Source lint covers CSS/JS; .mjs is not included by that upstream scanner. Read the template diff and rendered DOM separately.
