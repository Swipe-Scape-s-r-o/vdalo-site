## Platform

web

## Users

VDALO serves hotel owners and hotel teams in Ukraine. The implemented workflows address reception, housekeeping, guest preparation and the owner's view of hotel operations.

## Product Purpose

VDALO is software for hotel operations. It brings bookings, guest information and team work into a shared process. The website helps a visitor understand those workflows and contact the team for a demonstration.

## Operating Context

The team responds to a request, discusses the property's needs, demonstrates relevant scenarios and agrees the setup. Starting work includes help from the team. Do not promise free initial setup; the scope and terms of setup or migration are agreed separately.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed homepage topics include the booking calendar, guests, room readiness, housekeeping, rates, direct bookings, supported sales channels and reporting.
- Sales-channel compatibility and the data exchanged must be checked for the specific integration and property. Do not promise universal compatibility.
- `versions/v1/public/pricing-core.js` is authoritative for plans, prices, module availability and development status. Read it before making or changing price claims; do not use this document as a price table.
- AI assistant, PRRO and some other modules are still in development. Do not present them as released.
- The existing request forms, calculator and payment flow have their established handlers. The homepage's demo actions use the existing request form with demo context.
- The homepage calendar contains demonstration data. Its interactions do not change a visitor's bookings.
